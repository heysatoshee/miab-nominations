Why these awards exist

Bitcoin in Africa is being built by Africans, for Africans.

In classrooms and meetups, in open-source repos, at market stalls that take sats and at mining sites powered by local energy, the people doing this work rarely get the spotlight. These awards give it to them. Your nomination is how we find them.

Award categories 2026

Seven ways to honour impact.

Pick the category that fits best. We’ll take you straight to the form with it already selected. New this year: Builder, Product and Media Initiative of the Year.

01 Flagship · Individuals

The 21 Most Impactful African Bitcoiners

Our flagship list honours 21 people who moved Bitcoin adoption forward across Africa this year: educators, open-source builders, circular-economy organisers and community leaders.

02 Builders New

Builder of the Year

For the developer shipping open-source code, tools and infrastructure that make Bitcoin work better for Africans.

03 Projects & teams

Most Impactful African Project of the Year

For the initiative or programme that made a measurable difference to Bitcoin adoption, access or innovation.

04 Products New

Product of the Year

For the wallet, app, service or hardware that made Bitcoin easier, safer or more useful for Africans.

05 Communities

African Circular Economy of the Year

For communities living on a Bitcoin standard, where people earn, save, spend and learn in Bitcoin every day.

06 Media New

Media Initiative of the Year

For the podcast, publication, channel or campaign that informed and inspired Africans about Bitcoin this year.

African Bitcoin Miner of the Year

For home miners, teams and industrial operations securing the network while powering local energy and jobs.

Please read

Nominations open doors. Impact decides.

The list is not decided by how many nominations someone gets. Nominations help us discover people; the final selection is based on their actual contributions to the Bitcoin ecosystem. Quality of work matters more than quantity of votes.

A strong nomination

  • ✓
    Is specific. What did they do, where, and when?
  • ✓
    Shows proof. Links to events, repos, posts or press.
  • ✓
    Shows reach. Who benefited, and how many people?
  • ✓
    Is honest. One great nomination beats a hundred copies.
